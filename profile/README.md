## About

_AI for Materials_ is a course on how artificial intelligence is changing the way we understand, design and discover materials.
It covers how composition, structure and property information can be represented for machine learning.
You will build, train and evaluate your own models using open tools and public datasets.
Recent advances, from generative models to large language models and autonomous laboratories, are also explored.

The course is aimed at senior undergraduate and junior postgraduate students.
A basic working knowledge of Python is assumed.
